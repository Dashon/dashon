# The Future of Web Accessibility and AEO

In the age of Answer Engines, web accessibility takes on a new dimension. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is inherently linked to how accessible and machine-readable your content is.

## AEO is Accessibility for Machines
- **Semantic HTML**: Using tags like `<article>`, `<header>`, and `<section>` isn't just for screen readers—it's for AI crawlers trying to understand your site's hierarchy.
- **Structured Data**: JSON-LD is the ultimate accessibility tool for LLMs, providing explicit context about your content.
- **Alt Text and Captions**: High-quality descriptions help multimodal models (like GPT-4o or Gemini 1.5 Pro) "see" your content.

By focusing on accessibility, you're not just helping users with disabilities; you're ensuring your site is ready for the future of search.
