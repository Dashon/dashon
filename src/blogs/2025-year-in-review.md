# 2025 Year in Review: State of Full Stack Development

As 2025 comes to a close, it's time to look back at the technologies and trends that defined the year in full stack development.

## Highlights of 2025:
- **Agentic Orchestration**: The shift from "building features" to "training agents" within applications.
- **Serverless Evolution**: Cold starts are a thing of the past, with persistent serverless environments becoming the norm.
- **The Maturity of AEO**: Websites that didn't adapt to Answer Engines saw a significant drop in traffic.

It's been a year of rapid growth and even faster changes. I'm excited to see what 2026 brings to our industry.
