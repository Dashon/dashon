# Agentic AI: Beyond Simple Chatbots

In early 2026, the conversation has moved past simple chatbots. We are now building Agentic AI systems—autonomous entities that can reason, plan, and execute complex multi-step tasks.

## Characteristics of Agentic AI:
- **Autonomous Decision Making**: Agents that can choose which tool to use and when to use it based on a high-level goal.
- **Memory and Context**: Systems that remember past interactions and learn from them to improve future performance.
- **Self-Correction**: Agents that can detect their own mistakes and iterate until the task is completed correctly.

Building these systems requires a shift in mindset from traditional imperative programming to goal-oriented orchestration.
