# Decentralized Web: Myths vs Reality in 2026

The "Web3" hype of the early 2020s has settled into a more practical and decentralized reality in 2026. It's no longer just about tokens; it's about ownership and privacy.

## What's Real in 2026?
- **Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)**: Users owning their own identity and login data, independent of big tech platforms.
- **Distributed Storage**: Moving away from centralized cloud providers to resilient, distributed protocols for data hosting.
- **Peer-to-Peer Protocols**: Applications that function directly between users, reducing the need for central middlemen.

Decentralization is becoming a choice for privacy-conscious users and developers building resilient infrastructure.
