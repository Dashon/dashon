# Scalable Architecture for Digital Nomads

Being a digital nomad engineer requires a unique approach to productivity and systems design. When your "office" changes weekly, your infrastructure must be rock solid and easily accessible.

## The Portable Stack
- **Edge Computing**: Deploying logic closer to where you are, regardless of your physical location.
- **Local-First Development**: Ensuring you can keep working even with spotty Wi-Fi, syncing only when a connection is stable.
- **Cloud-Native Automation**: Letting CI/CD pipelines handle the heavy lifting while you focus on code.

In this post, I share my experience building scalable systems while traveling the world, and how you can apply these principles to any distributed team.
