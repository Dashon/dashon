# Personal Branding for Senior Engineers

In a competitive and rapidly changing market, your personal brand is your most valuable asset. It's not about being an "influencer"; it's about being known for your expertise and your perspective.

## Building Your Brand:
- **Write Consistently**: Sharing your knowledge through blogs and articles establishes you as a thought leader.
- **Contribute to Open Source**: Your code is your best portfolio.
- **Speak Your Truth**: Don't just follow trends; share your unique insights and experiences from the field.

Your personal brand is what gets you into the room where the most interesting problems are solved.
