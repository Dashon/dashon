# Sustainable Software Engineering Practices

As the environmental impact of large AI models and global data centers becomes clearer, sustainability is becoming a key metric for software engineering teams.

## Principles of Green Code:
- **Efficiency First**: Reducing the computational cost of your algorithms and queries saves both time and energy.
- **Lean Infrastructure**: Moving away from oversized "always-on" clusters to optimized, on-demand resources.
- **Conscious AI Usage**: Using the right model for the job, rather than the largest one available, to minimize carbon footprint.

Sustainable engineering isn't just good for the planet; it's good business.
